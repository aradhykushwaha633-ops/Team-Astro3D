Why COSMOSIS exists

Space science is the subject that makes students curious about STEM, yet most online material is English-only, video-heavy, and assumes fast internet and one device per learner. COSMOSIS is built for Class 6–12 classrooms where that is not true.

In the student’s language

15 Indian languages across every page. Translation needs internet, and text drawn inside 3D scenes stays in English.

Works when connectivity doesn’t

Save the site for offline use after one visit, and print quiz worksheets with answer keys for rooms with no devices.

Learning you can measure

Mission quizzes, XP and streaks, seven skill challenges and a certificate, so progress is visible to the student.

Ready for the teacher

The Teacher Hub gives lesson plans, classroom activities, worksheets and a live team quiz without extra preparation.
